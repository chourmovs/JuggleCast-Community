# Third-party notices

[Repository home](README.md) · [Documentation index](docs/README.md)

FlowCast Community relies on independent projects including those named in [Acknowledgements](ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.md). Their copyrights, licences, notices and trademark rules remain their own.

This page is intentionally not a hand-maintained exhaustive dependency inventory. For a particular release, inspect the SBOM, licence labels and notices attached to that release and its OCI images in the [public release record](https://github.com/chourmovs/JuggleCast-Community/releases). If a release does not publish that evidence, do not infer an image licence from this repository's MIT licence.

Redistributors must review the exact release artifacts they distribute, preserve applicable notices and satisfy the corresponding source, attribution and other licence obligations.
